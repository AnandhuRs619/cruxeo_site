FAQs

#### Frequently Asked Questions

##### What does CRUXEO do?

CRUXEO is a Dubai creative technology company. Through four divisions we build interactive event experiences (NEXPLAY), produce film and display content (LUMEN LAB), run drone and fireworks shows (IGNISPECTRA), and create AI tools and software (SYNAPSE).

##### Do I need to work with all four divisions?

No. You can work with one division or combine several. When a project crosses divisions, you get one lead contact and one coordinated proposal.

##### How do I start a project?

Call us or connect with us on WhatsApp (+971 54 545 4891), or email info@cruxeo.com. We reply with the next step and the right person from our team.

##### Where do you work?

We are based in Dubai and deliver projects across the United Arab Emirates and wider region.

##### How much does it cost?

Costs depend on the project scale, duration, and hardware requirements, so we quote after receiving your brief. Share your project details to get a clear quotation.

##### Do drone light shows and pyrotechnics require permits?

Yes, shows usually require permits and venue approval. The team coordinates these and confirms feasibility for each project.
