### Creative Technology That Brings People Together

CRUXEO Experience brings technology, creativity and production together to help brands connect with people and bring ideas to life across Dubai and the UAE.

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Our Model

#### Four Divisions. One Coordinated Delivery.

CRUXEO Experience brings technology, creativity and production together to help brands connect with people and bring ideas to life. Through NEXPLAY, LUMEN LAB, IGNISPECTRA and SYNAPSE, we shape interactive experiences, create visual content and environments, produce spectacular shows, and build intelligent digital products.

Clients can engage one specialist division or bring several together around one brief, with a clear scope and coordinated delivery from first concept to show day.

###### One team to brief

Clients can engage one specialist division or bring several together around one brief with a single technical lead.

###### Clear scope and costs

Itemized quotes with predictable hardware, content, and on-site crew costs. No hidden fees or unverified add-ons.

###### Technology tested before show day

100% bench-tested hardware, calibrated displays, rehearsed flight routines, and verified software.

Creative technology and production systems trusted for live events and venues
