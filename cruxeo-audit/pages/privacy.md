### Privacy Policy

Conforming to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and international data governance standards.

#### 1. Introduction & Scope

CRUXEO Experience (“CRUXEO”, “we”, “us”) operates as a creative technology company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, delivering services across four specialist divisions: NEXPLAY, LUMEN LAB, IGNISPECTRA, and SYNAPSE. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard business and technical information submitted through our website (cruxeo.com) and associated digital enquiry channels.

#### 2. Information We Collect

We collect information solely to review, scope, and quote requested event productions and technical projects:

- Contact Credentials: Name, business email address, phone number or WhatsApp contact handle.

- Project Brief Details: Event type, target venue, anticipated dates, attendance figures, and technical rider specifications.

- Technical Files & Slates: Architecture diagrams, floorplans, CAD drawings, or RFP documents voluntarily attached to enquiry submissions.

- Assistant Conversations: Interactions with our technical AI assistant are processed in transient memory to help formulate briefs and are saved only upon explicit user submission.

#### 3. Lawful Basis & Data Purpose

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, all processing is conducted under the lawful basis of contractual necessity (evaluating technical briefs, calculating engineering feasibility, preparing itemized commercial proposals) and legitimate business interest. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or exchange client data with third-party advertisers.

#### 4. Data Retention & On-Premise Privacy

For live event activations executed by our SYNAPSE division (such as AI photobooths, generative media kiosks, and RFID leaderboards), all neural processing is performed locally on on-premise GPU workstation racks. Guest portrait data and photos are processed in volatile memory with zero unauthorized cloud storage, ensuring complete attendee confidentiality.

#### 5. Security, Access & Inquiries

All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS with TLS 1.3 and strict Content-Security-Policy headers. If you have questions regarding your data or wish to request data deletion, contact our data protection team directly at  info@cruxeo.com  or call  +971 54 545 4891 .
