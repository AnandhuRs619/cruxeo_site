Our Model

Four Divisions. One Coordinated Delivery.

CRUXEO Experience brings technology, creativity and production together to help brands connect with people and bring ideas to life. Through NEXPLAY, LUMEN LAB, IGNISPECTRA and SYNAPSE, we shape interactive experiences, create visual content and environments, produce spectacular shows, and build intelligent digital products.

Clients can engage one specialist division or bring several together around one brief, with a clear scope and coordinated delivery from first concept to show day.
