#### Frequently Asked Questions

##### What does CRUXEO do?

CRUXEO is a Dubai creative technology company. Through four divisions we build interactive event experiences (NEXPLAY), produce film and display content (LUMEN LAB), run drone and fireworks shows (IGNISPECTRA), and create AI tools and software (SYNAPSE).

##### Do I need to work with all four divisions?

No. You can work with one division or combine several. When a project crosses divisions, you get one lead contact and one coordinated proposal.

##### How do I start a project?

Call us or connect with us on WhatsApp (+971 54 545 4891), or email info@cruxeo.com. We reply with the next step and the right person from our team.

##### Where do you work?

We are based in Dubai and deliver projects across the United Arab Emirates and wider region.

##### How much does it cost?

Costs depend on the project scale, duration, and hardware requirements, so we quote after receiving your brief. Share your project details to get a clear quotation.

##### Do drone light shows and pyrotechnics require permits?

Yes, shows usually require permits and venue approval. The team coordinates these and confirms feasibility for each project.

### Terms & Conditions

Standard commercial terms, equipment rental riders, aviation clearances, and production delivery commitments.

#### 1. Scope of Engagement

These Terms and Conditions govern all proposals, production riders, equipment rentals, show designs, and custom software delivery executed by CRUXEO Experience and its divisions (NEXPLAY, LUMEN LAB, IGNISPECTRA, SYNAPSE). Specific technical scopes are formally documented in an agreed Work Order or Project Rider signed by both parties.

#### 2. Quotations, Scoping & Pricing

Due to venue-specific structural rigging limits, electrical distribution requirements, municipal permitting, and staffing days, all initial quotations are estimates based on provided briefs. Formal binding contracts require a signed statement of work following site inspection and electrical load verification.

#### 3. Drone Shows & Aviation Clearances (IGNISPECTRA)

Drone swarm spectacles are subject to regulatory clearance from the General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA) and/or Dubai Civil Aviation Authority (DCAA), along with local police security approvals. A minimum lead time of 4 weeks (8-12 weeks for major UAE holiday dates) is required. Operations adhere to strict safety parameters: 75m to 100m audience standoff distance and sustained wind limits up to 18-20 knots (~35 km/h).

#### 4. Hardware Hire & Technical Riders (LUMEN LAB / NEXPLAY)

Equipment hire includes delivery, professional rigging by certified technicians, and operation during show hours. Clients are responsible for ensuring adequate three-phase power supply, safe load-in dock access, and venue security during overnight staging.

#### 5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the federal laws of the United Arab Emirates and the local laws applicable in the Emirate of Dubai. For any commercial or contractual inquiries, reach our commercial desk at  info@cruxeo.com .
